## Core PM Skills (luôn có sẵn)

### 1. Project Charter
**Cấu trúc:** Thông tin DA → Mục tiêu → Phạm vi → Stakeholders → Milestones → Ngân sách → Rủi ro → Phê duyệt

### 2. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
Phân rã deliverables thành work packages. Rule: 100% rule — tổng WP = 100% scope.

### 3. Risk Management
**Risk Register:** ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Status
**Risk Heat Map:** Probability × Impact → 🔴 High / 🟡 Medium / 🟢 Low

### 4. RACI Matrix
| Task | PM | PO | BA | DEV | QA |
**R**esponsible | **A**ccountable | **C**onsulted | **I**nformed

### 5. Budget & Cost Tracker
**Categories:** Personnel | Infrastructure | Licenses | Training | Contingency

### 6. Stakeholder Register & Communication Plan
Power × Interest matrix → Communication frequency & channel per stakeholder

### 7. Tailoring Decision Record
**Mục đích:** Ghi lại WHY team đã chọn adapt methodology (Scrum/Kanban/hybrid).
