# Kỹ Năng: Agile Ceremonies & Sprint Management

---

## 1. Sprint Planning
**Input:** Refined backlog + Team capacity + Sprint goal
**Output:** Sprint backlog + Commitment + Dependencies identified
**Timebox:** 2h per sprint week (2-week sprint = 4h max)

## 2. Daily Standup
**3 câu hỏi:** Hôm qua làm gì? | Hôm nay sẽ làm gì? | Có blocker nào?
**PM action:** Track blockers → Escalate nếu >24h unresolved

## 3. Sprint Review (Demo)
**Format:** Sprint goal recap → Demo → Feedback capture → Updated backlog
**Stakeholders:** PO + Team + Business stakeholders

## 4. Retrospective
**Techniques:** Start/Stop/Continue | 4Ls (Liked/Learned/Lacked/Longed For) | Sailboat
**Output:** Top 3 action items → Assign owner → Track next sprint

## 5. Velocity Tracker
| Sprint | Planned SP | Completed SP | Velocity | Notes |
**Use:** Chỉ dùng cho capacity planning, KHÔNG dùng để so sánh team.

## 6. Resource Capacity Planner
**Công thức:** Available hours = (Working days × Hours/day) × (1 - Meeting% - PTO%) × Team size
**Buffer:** 80% capacity rule (20% cho interrupt, tech debt)

## 7. Lessons Learned Facilitation
**Quy trình:** Collect data → Generate insights → Decide actions → Close
**PM Role:** Facilitate, KHÔNG judge. Tạo safe space.
