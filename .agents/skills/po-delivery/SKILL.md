## D4: Make an Impact (15%)

### Product Vision & Strategy
**Vision Board:** Vision (3-5 năm) + Target Group + Needs + North Star Metric
**Strategic Roadmap:** NOW (Committed) → NEXT (Directional) → LATER (Themes only)

### Product Metrics & OKR
**North Star Metric:** 1 metric duy nhất phản ánh giá trị cốt lõi
**AARRR:** Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue
**HEART:** Happiness | Engagement | Adoption | Retention | Task Success

### Impact Mapping (Gojko Adzic)
```
WHY (Business Goal) → WHO (Actors) → HOW (Impacts) → WHAT (Deliverables)
```
**Nguyên tắc:** "Không build feature nào không trace được về business goal."

### Business Cases (Mini)
Mini business case cho individual features — justify investment.
**Template:** Problem → Solution → Cost → Benefits → ROI → Risk → Timeline

### Decision Modelling
**Decision Matrix** (weighted criteria) | **Decision Tree** (outcomes + probabilities) | **6 Thinking Hats**

## D5: Deliver Often (15%)

### Backlog Management
**3 Cấp:** Epic → Feature → User Story → Task
**INVEST:** Independent | Negotiable | Valuable | Estimable | Small | Testable
**Story Splitting:** SPIDR (Spike, Path, Interface, Data, Rules)

### Story Mapping (Jeff Patton)
```
[Activities] (backbone) → [Tasks] (walking skeleton) → [Stories] (details)
── Release 1 line ── Release 2 line ──
```
**Nguyên tắc:** "Cắt ngang (thin slice) thay vì cắt dọc (full feature)."

### Job Stories
**Format:** "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]"
**Dùng khi:** "As a user" quá generic, context quan trọng hơn role

### Release Management
**Types:** Big Bang | Incremental | Feature Toggle | Canary
**Checklist:** Pre-Launch → Post-Launch Monitoring (W1) → Post-Launch Review (W4)

### Definition Concepts (DoR / DoV / DoD)
- **DoR:** Story sẵn sàng vào sprint?
- **DoV:** Increment sẵn sàng review/demo? (NEW từ POA)
- **DoD:** Feature hoàn tất và có thể release?

### Conceptualizing Solution
**Mức độ:** Sketch (PO) → Wireframe (UX) → Mockup (UX) → Prototype (UX+Dev)
PO wireframe khi concept phức tạp, cần align mental model.
