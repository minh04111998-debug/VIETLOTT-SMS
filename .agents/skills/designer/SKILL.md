## Core Designer Skills (luôn có sẵn)

### 1. Wireframing & Mockup
**Levels:** Sketch → Low-fi Wireframe → High-fi Mockup → Interactive Prototype
**Tools:** Figma (primary), Sketch, Adobe XD

### 2. User Flow Mapping
Visualize user journey qua screens. Dùng Mermaid flowchart cho nhanh, Figma cho chi tiết.

### 3. Animation & Interaction Guidelines
**Nguyên tắc:** Purposeful (không animation vô nghĩa) | Responsive (<300ms) | Consistent | Accessible
**Easing:** ease-out cho entrance, ease-in cho exit, ease-in-out cho transition
