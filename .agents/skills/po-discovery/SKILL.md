## D1: Apply Foundational Concepts (10%)

### PO Type Assessment
**3 loại PO:** Pre-PMF → Discovery PO | Post-PMF → Delivery PO | Growth/Mature → Growth PO
**Context check:** Product-based (continuous) vs Project-based (fixed scope)

### Product Lifecycle Management
**Stages:** Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline
**Nguyên tắc:** "Strategy phụ thuộc lifecycle stage. Growth tactics trong Decline stage = lãng phí."

### Organizational Readiness
Đánh giá: Culture, Structure, Skills, Processes, Leadership support
**Output:** Readiness Assessment + Transformation Roadmap

### Technology Awareness
PO cần hiểu: Frontend/Backend/API | Tech Debt (~20% sprint) | Build vs Buy vs Partner
→ Chi tiết: `../po/references/tech-awareness.md`

## D2: Cultivate Customer Intimacy (15%)

### Market Intelligence & Competitive Analysis
**Quy trình:** Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) → Competitive Mapping → Feature Parity → Go/No-Go
**Nguyên tắc:** "Feature table dùng để QUYẾT, không dùng để COPY."

### User Journey & Customer Development
**3 loại Journey:** Goal-based | Pain Point & Friction Map | Moment of Truth
**Interview:** Dùng câu hỏi Jobs-to-be-Done, KHÔNG dùng leading/hypothetical questions

### Empathy Maps
**4 Quadrants:** Think & Feel | See | Hear | Say & Do + Pains / Gains
**Khi nào dùng:** Trước khi viết User Story, sau Customer Interview

### Value Proposition Canvas
**2 phần:** Customer Profile (Jobs, Pains, Gains) ↔ Value Map (Products, Pain Relievers, Gain Creators)
**Fit:** Pain Relievers address Pains VÀ Gain Creators address Gains = Product-Market Fit

### Product-Market Fit
**Sean Ellis Test:** "Bạn sẽ cảm thấy thế nào nếu không còn dùng sản phẩm này?"
**Ngưỡng:** ≥ 40% "Rất thất vọng" = đã đạt PMF

### Human-Centred Design for Products
**5 phases:** Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test
**PO Role:** PO lead Empathize + Define. Designer lead Ideate + Prototype. Cùng Test.

### Focus Groups
Thu thập insight từ nhóm users (6-10 người). Dùng khi cần nhiều perspectives. KHÔNG dùng khi cần deep personal stories.

## D3: Engage the Whole Team (15%)

### Stakeholder Management
**Map:** Power × Interest matrix → Manage Closely / Keep Satisfied / Keep Informed
**Conflict Resolution:** Document → Data check → Propose compromise → Escalate → Document decision

### Cross-functional Collaboration
**Dual-Track Agile:** Discovery track (PO + Designer + BA) song song Delivery track (Dev + QA)
**Three Amigos:** PO + Dev + QA review mỗi story trước sprint

### Collaborative Games
Buy a Feature | Speedboat | Product Box | Prune the Product Tree

### Shared PO Responsibilities
**Model:** Chief PO → Feature POs → Team POs (SAFe context)
**Nguyên tắc:** "1 backlog = 1 PO accountable. Nhưng analysis work = shared."
